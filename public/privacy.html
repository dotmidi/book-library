Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 (optional sharing with friends, CD and vinyl lookups)

Book Library is a small personal app for keeping a list of your books. It is designed so that your data stays with you, in your own Google account.

What the app can access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

  • Files this app creates in your Google Drive (drive.file). The app creates one spreadsheet called “Book Library” and only reads and writes that spreadsheet. It cannot see, open or change any of your other files.
  • A small private settings file (drive.appdata), hidden in your Drive, where the app remembers which spreadsheet is yours so it can find it again on your other devices.

The app also sees the email address of the account you sign in with, to show you which account is in use.

Where your data is stored

  • Your book list is stored in the spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. You own it, and you can open, edit, download or delete it at any time.
  • For speed, a copy of your list is kept in your browser’s storage on each device you use. Signing out removes it.
  • There is no server and no database run by the app’s maker. Nobody else receives a copy of your list.

Book information lookups

To fill in book details, the app sends the ISBN (the number under the barcode) to public book catalogues: Open Library and Google Books for all books; openBD and the National Diet Library for Japanese books; the Bibliothèque nationale de France for French books; and Crossref when no other catalogue knows the book. For CDs and records, the barcode is sent to MusicBrainz, and covers come from the Cover Art Archive. Only the ISBN is sent, never your name, email or library. Cover images are loaded from these services.

Sharing with friends (optional, off by default)

  • In the Friends tab you can turn on Share my library. This sets your spreadsheet to “anyone with the link can view” in Google Drive and gives you a link to send to friends. Anyone who has the link can see everything in your sheet, including notes and the names of people you lent books to. They cannot change anything.
  • The name you type for friends is stored in your sheet’s _meta tab so friends can see it.
  • Friends you add are listed in a hidden _friends tab of your own sheet.
  • Turning sharing off removes the link permission; the link stops working immediately.
  • Friends’ libraries are read with the app’s Google API key, not with your Google account.

No tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising, no cookies of its own and no tracking of any kind.

Removing access

You can sign out in the app under More → Sign out. To remove the app’s access completely, visit your Google Account permissions and remove “Book Library”. Your spreadsheet stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Use of Google user data

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to show and edit your own book list.
